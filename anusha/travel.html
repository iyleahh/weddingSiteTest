Arrival notes

A few simple reminders to make the trip feel easier from the moment you land.

Airport

Plan to arrive at Jorge Chávez International Airport and arrange transportation onward to Miraflores. We are gathering information about your travel plans in the RSVP to help facilitate a smooth arrival.

Timing

If you are able, arriving a little early will give you time to rest, adjust, and enjoy Lima before the celebrations begin.

Getting around

Most guests should be able to rely on hotel transport, rideshare, or arranged wedding transportation once details are shared.
